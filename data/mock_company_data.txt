Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA) statutory net profit after tax (NPAT) decreased 6% to $9,482 million for the full fiscal year.
The banking group announced a fully franked final dividend of $2.50 per share, bringing the total dividend allocation to $4.65.
Net interest margin (NIM) compressed by 8 basis points to 1.99% due to intense domestic home loan competition and deposit switching.
APRA prudential standards require the bank to maintain a Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio well above the 10.25% regulatory minimum.
The financial group deployed an internal model assessment verifying institutional credit risk profiles against sovereign debt defaults.
